MIKU WINKEL


From the fourth round of the Hatsune Miku x GSC Illustration Contest comes a 1/7 scale figure of Sakura Miku in her official outfit, based on the illustration by fusuma. The charming illustration, which captures Miku in an ephemeral moment, has been carefully brought to life with careful attention paid to preserving the atmosphere of the original artwork. Translucent parts have been used to sculpt the balloons and hair, giving the figure a rich and visually interesting appearance. Be sure to add the adorable yet ephemeral Sakura Miku to your collection!
